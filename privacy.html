Privacy Policy for minpro

Effective date: October 16, 2026

minpro respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the minpro iOS app.

1. Data You Create in minpro

minpro allows you to create and manage information related to your knitting and crochet projects. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • project names and project details
  • notes
  • yarn information
  • tools and needle information
  • stitch counters
  • project status and dates
  • images and PDF attachments
  • knitting activity and statistics

This information is created by you for use within the app.

2. Local Storage and iCloud

Your minpro data is stored on your device using Apple system technologies.

If iCloud synchronization is available and enabled for minpro, applicable app data may also be stored and synchronized using Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services.

minpro uses the user's private CloudKit database for synchronized app data. Your use of iCloud is also subject to Apple's terms and privacy policies.

3. Data Collected by the Developer

minpro does not operate its own user account system, analytics backend, advertising system, or tracking service.

The developer of minpro does not receive the project content, notes, images, PDFs, knitting activity, or other content you create inside the app through a developer-operated server.

minpro does not sell your personal information and does not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

4. Analytics and Tracking

minpro does not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics services. minpro does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

5. In-App Purchases

minpro may offer a Pro upgrade through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Purchases are processed by Apple.

minpro may access StoreKit transaction and entitlement information necessary to determine whether Pro features have been purchased. The developer does not receive your payment card or other payment credentials.

6. Third-Party Services

minpro currently relies on Apple platform services including:

  • Apple iCloud and CloudKit for synchronization
  • Apple StoreKit and the App Store for in-app purchases

These services are operated by Apple and are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted through minpro, removed as part of normal app use, or the app and its local data are removed from the device.

If iCloud synchronization is enabled, synchronized data may also remain in your private iCloud database. Deleting minpro from one device does not necessarily delete data already synchronized to iCloud.

If you have questions about deleting data associated with minpro, contact the developer using the address below.

8. Security

minpro relies on Apple's platform security, sandboxing, data storage, iCloud, and StoreKit technologies to protect app data.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if minpro's features, data handling, or third-party services change. The effective date above will be updated when this policy changes.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or questions about your data: justsayjakob@icloud.com
