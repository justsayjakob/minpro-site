minpro Support

If you have questions, encounter a problem, or would like to provide feedback about minpro, you can contact the developer by email.

justsayjakob@icloud.com

When contacting support

If you are reporting a problem, it can be helpful to include:

  • your iPhone or iPad model
  • your iOS or iPadOS version
  • a short description of what happened
  • the steps that led to the issue

Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary to resolve your support request.
